## 📈 What Trade Ideas Brings to Trading Workflows

Trade Ideas is a market analysis and stock screening platform designed to help traders examine market activity and identify instruments that match selected criteria. Its tools support structured research by bringing screening, alerts, and analytical features into a dedicated trading workspace.

Stock screening is one of the platform's central capabilities. Users can configure filters around market behaviour and other available data points to narrow a broad set of instruments into a more manageable research list.

Customisable scans help users organise research according to their own criteria. Different scan configurations can be used to examine changing price activity, volume conditions, and other measurable characteristics supported by the platform.

Holly AI provides algorithm-assisted market analysis features intended to help users examine potential trading opportunities. Its outputs should be treated as research information rather than guarantees of future market performance.

Market monitoring tools help users follow changing conditions throughout a trading session. Alerts and scanning results can draw attention to instruments that meet specified criteria, reducing the need to inspect every stock manually.

Trading signals and technical conditions can provide a starting point for further investigation. Users can review the context behind an alert, examine price behaviour, and apply their own risk-management rules before making decisions.

Historical analysis and supported simulation capabilities help users assess how a strategy might have behaved under past market conditions. Historical results have limitations and do not establish that a strategy will perform similarly in future markets.

Watchlists and organised research routines make it easier to keep track of selected instruments. Traders can group stocks according to their interests, compare observations, and revisit candidates as market conditions change.

The platform can support different research styles, including intraday screening and broader stock analysis. The usefulness of each workflow depends on the user's objectives, chosen filters, and the data available through their account.

Trade Ideas may also offer features for connecting analysis with trading workflows, depending on the selected configuration and supported services. Users should verify current compatibility and account requirements before relying on any integration.

For active market research, a consistent screening process can help users apply the same criteria across multiple instruments. Maintaining clear rules makes it easier to compare results and avoid changing the research process without a defined reason.

Reporting and analytical views can help users review observations and assess the consistency of their approach. Keeping notes about selected criteria, market conditions, and research outcomes can support a more disciplined evaluation process.

Access to specific features, data services, and simulation capabilities may vary by subscription, configuration, and supported market. Users should review the current product details to determine which tools are available for their intended workflow.

Overall, Trade Ideas provides a specialised environment for stock screening and market research. Its tools can help organise information and surface instruments for further analysis, but they cannot remove market uncertainty or guarantee profitable outcomes.
